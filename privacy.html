Privacy Policy

Johnny TEC AI Reply · Last updated: October 2026

Johnny TEC AI Reply ("the App", "we") is a private WhatsApp assistant that helps its owner answer messages sent to a WhatsApp Business number using artificial intelligence. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • WhatsApp messages: the text of messages that people send to the App's WhatsApp Business number, and the replies sent back.
  • Phone numbers and names: the sender's WhatsApp phone number and the profile or display name that WhatsApp provides.
  • Conversation notes: short notes about how a sender writes (for example, their language and tone), created to write better replies.
  • Technical logs: timestamps, message status, and error records used to keep the service working.

2. How we use information

  • To receive messages and send replies through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • To generate reply suggestions with an AI model.
  • To show conversation history to the App's owner.
  • To fix errors and keep the service reliable.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Services that process data

To work, the App relies on these providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) delivers messages.
  • Google (Gemini API) receives message text to write replies.
  • Supabase stores conversations, contacts, and settings.
  • Render hosts the App's server.

4. Data retention

Messages and contact records are kept until the App's owner deletes them or until a deletion request (see below) is completed.

5. Data deletion and your rights

If you have messaged the App's WhatsApp number and want your data deleted, send an email to YOUR_EMAIL_HERE with the phone number you used and the subject "Delete my data". We will delete your messages, contact record, and notes within 30 days. You may also ask what data we hold about you.

6. Security

Access to the App's dashboard requires a private owner login. Secret keys are stored as server environment variables and are never placed in the public website. No online system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
